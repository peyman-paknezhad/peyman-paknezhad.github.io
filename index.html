About Me

I'm a Master's student in the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in AI for Image Processing & Computer Vision (IPCVai), studying across Budapest, Madrid and now Bordeaux. Before that, I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science at Amirkabir University of Technology.

My work sits where deep learning meets trust. I attack and defend vision models, from 3D Gaussian Splatting scenes to prototype-based classifiers, and I use interpretability tools such as sparse autoencoders and Shapley values to open the black box.

Right now I'm a research intern on the MULTISCOPE project at Instituto Superior Técnico, working on explainable AI and prototypical learning for Stimulated Raman Scattering data. I'm always happy to collaborate on meaningful research.

Selected Projects

Urban Elements ReID — IEEE ICIP 2026 Grand Challenge

Cross-camera re-identification of urban objects (containers, crosswalks, bins and traffic signs), where the test camera was never seen in training. Our solution fine-tunes DINOv3 ViT-H+ in stages with ArcFace and triplet losses, then applies per-class k-reciprocal re-ranking.

2nd Place in the IEEE ICIP 2026 Grand Challenge
Re-IDDINOv3ViT-H+Metric LearningRe-ranking

Do 3D Gaussian Splatting Watermarks Survive Compression?

Showed that a standard compression pipeline (pruning, SH quantisation, fine-tuning) erases GaussianMarker- and GuardSplat-style watermarks, and explained why: carriers are the Gaussians pruning removes first.

96–99% watermark retention after 66% pruning with importance-aware carriers
3DGSWatermarkingCompressiongsplat

Same Prediction, Different Explanation: Attacking PIP-Net

Imperceptible PGD perturbations keep PIP-Net's prediction but move or replace its prototype evidence. A prototype-consistency regulariser makes the attack about 3× harder at almost no accuracy cost.

83% → 29% move-attack success with the regulariser, at −0.4 pt accuracy
XAIPrototypesPGDCUB-200

What Do DINOv3, CLIP and I-JEPA “See”?

Trained TopK sparse autoencoders on ViT patch features and used COCO masks to compare concept dictionaries. DINOv3 is object-level, CLIP is category-level and I-JEPA is mostly part-level.

~2× more object-level concepts in DINOv3 than in CLIP
Sparse AutoencodersMech. Interp.DINOv3CLIP

Which Frames Does a Video LLM Use? Exact Frame Shapley

Exact Shapley values and pairwise interactions over input frames for Qwen2.5-VL give zero-shot timestamped captions and reveal that the model largely treats a video as an unordered bag of frames.

86% of videos get the same answer played forwards or backwards
